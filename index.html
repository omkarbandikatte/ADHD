ADHD EEG Biomarkers

The scientifically validated EEG signatures the model looks for.

4 – 8 Hz

Theta Band ↑ Elevated in ADHD

Frontal theta power is consistently increased in ADHD. Associated with inattention, working memory deficits, and reduced cortical arousal.

13 – 30 Hz

Beta Band ↓ Reduced in ADHD

Beta power is reduced, especially in frontal regions. Beta is linked to active concentration, alertness, and cognitive control — areas ADHD affects most.

3.8 Θ/β
Ratio > 3.0

Theta / Beta Ratio Key Biomarker

The TBR is the most clinically established ADHD biomarker. Values above 3.0 strongly suggest ADHD. The model treats this as a primary feature.

8 – 13 Hz

Alpha Band ↓ Lower Amplitude

Alpha suppression during tasks is reduced in ADHD. Normal brains show strong alpha suppression when focusing — ADHD brains show weaker modulation.

L vs R frontal

Frontal Asymmetry Altered Pattern

Left-right alpha and theta asymmetry in the frontal lobe (Fp1/Fp2, F3/F4) is disrupted in ADHD, correlating with emotional dysregulation and attention control.

Complexity

Sample Entropy ↓ Reduced Complexity

EEG complexity (measured by sample entropy) is lower in ADHD, suggesting more regular, less adaptive neural dynamics — particularly during cognitive tasks.

Backend Pipeline

Signal Processing Pipeline

Every step the system takes from raw .mat data to feature vectors.

1. Load .mat File

scipy.io.loadmat() — Reads EEG matrix, sampling rate, and channel labels. Auto-detects variable names (EEG, data, signal).

2. Bandpass Filter

0.5 – 40 Hz Butterworth filter removes DC drift and high-frequency noise. Notch filter at 50/60 Hz eliminates power-line interference.

3. Artifact Removal

Threshold-based rejection removes eye-blink (EOG) and muscle (EMG) artifacts. Amplitude threshold: ±100 µV. Optional ICA decomposition.

4. Epoching

Signal is segmented into 2-second, 50%-overlapping epochs. Each epoch becomes an independent sample for feature extraction.

5. Feature Extraction

Welch's PSD computes band powers. Coherence matrices capture inter-channel synchrony. Hjorth parameters, entropy, and asymmetry indices are computed per epoch.

6. Feature Aggregation

Epoch-level features are aggregated (mean ± std) into a single 42-dimensional feature vector representing the entire recording session.

7. ML Classification

The feature vector is passed to the trained Random Forest or SVM classifier. Output: ADHD probability [0–1] + feature importance via SHAP.

What are SHAP values?

SHAP is a game-theory-based method that assigns each feature a "responsibility score" for the model's prediction. It answers: "How much did elevated frontal theta power contribute to the ADHD diagnosis?"

Clinical Interpretation

The dashboard displays SHAP waterfall charts, force plots, and summary beeswarms — making it easy for neurologists to validate whether the model's reasoning aligns with clinical knowledge.

Not a Black Box

Every prediction can be traced back to specific EEG channels and frequency bands. Professors and clinicians can challenge, verify, and trust the output.
